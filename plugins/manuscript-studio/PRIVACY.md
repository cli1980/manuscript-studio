# Manuscript Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

## Scope and operator

Crossborder Art & Media LLC distributes Manuscript Studio, maintained by Lin
Shaw, as skills and local helper scripts. This policy covers the skills-only
plugin and standalone skills, not the separately operated workflow prototype,
your AI provider, GitHub, or third-party tools.

The plugin has no publisher-operated manuscript server, account system,
advertising, analytics, or telemetry endpoint. Installing it does not itself
upload manuscripts to the publisher. Your AI host still receives and processes
the conversations, attachments, tool calls, and artifacts you use with it.

## Data used and purposes

At your direction, the skills may process manuscript text and attachments;
author/contributor names and credits; titles, languages, genres and other work
metadata; editorial notes, translations, synopses and query materials; and
revision identifiers, hashes, approval decisions, and local file locations.
These are used to prepare, edit, translate, format, research, or review the
specific work you request, and to retain reviewable state and provenance.

Provide only the manuscript scope needed for the task. Do not provide passwords,
API keys, payment-card data, government identifiers, protected health information,
or unnecessary sensitive personal information. Redact real-person sensitive
details before use; avoid confidential manuscripts unless your host and tools
are authorized to process them.

## Recipients and third-party processing

Your chosen AI host/model provider processes content under its own terms and
privacy policy. Enabled filesystem, document, rendering, browser, search, and
storage tools may receive the content necessary for your requested task.
Agent research may send search queries or visit third-party sites. Check those
tools' permissions and policies; the plugin does not make them local or private.
Review and minimize identifying details before external research.

The publisher does not receive manuscript content automatically, sell it,
use it for advertising, or operate a training-data collection service.
Content you voluntarily submit to support is received through GitHub and is
visible according to the issue's visibility. Do not post manuscripts or private
personal information in public issues.

## Retention

There is no publisher-side manuscript retention period because the skills
do not send manuscripts to a publisher service. Files, review receipts,
renderings, and copies created in your project or host artifacts remain until
you delete them or the host applies its retention policy. Uninstalling the
plugin does not delete those files, chat history, backups, or Git history.
Your AI provider, external tools, and GitHub have independent retention rules.
Public support posts can remain public indefinitely unless removed through
GitHub's available controls; copies, notifications, and platform backups may
remain subject to GitHub's policy.

## Controls and contact

You can limit accessible folders and attachments, decline tool permissions,
review and reject proposed changes, stop a workflow, uninstall the skills, and
delete local artifacts. Use your host's controls for conversation retention,
model-training preferences, export, and deletion. Git history and backups
require separate attention when removing sensitive content.

For support or privacy questions, contact the maintainer through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/cli1980/manuscript-studio/issues).
For a sensitive concern, post only a request for a private contact route,
without the sensitive details. A dedicated private support mailbox is not
currently published.

Policy changes will be recorded here with an updated date. Review this policy
and your host's policy before installing or updating the plugin.
