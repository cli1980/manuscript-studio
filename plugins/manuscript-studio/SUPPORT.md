# Support

Contact the Manuscript Studio maintainer, Lin Shaw, through the public
[GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/cli1980/manuscript-studio/issues).
This is the support contact declared in the plugin manifest.

Include your host/product and version, plugin version, operating system,
affected skill, available tools, a sanitized error, and minimal reproduction
steps. Synthetic text is preferable to a real manuscript. Do not include
credentials, identifying personal information, confidential manuscripts, or
full workspaces. Ask for a private contact route before sharing a sensitive
privacy concern. No private mailbox or response-time guarantee is published.

Check the [runtime support guide](RUNTIME-SUPPORT.md) first for missing tools,
blocked formatting, or text approvals when inline review is unavailable.
Data practices and deletion controls are described in the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

Installing from a local/Git marketplace is not public-directory publication.
Before submitting a release, ensure the manifest's website, support, and privacy
URLs are publicly readable at the published revision. Repository changes alone
do not publish a privacy policy or establish directory approval.
