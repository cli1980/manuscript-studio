April 30th, 9:00 AM

“So flashy, Peng, are you really going to get this done?”

“Yeah, the holiday’s coming up soon, and I can’t stand this crappy job for even a second longer. It just so happens there’s this hairdressing department opened up, so I’m getting a haircut here early today. After work tonight, I’m going to party hard and show you all something to envy.”

“But I heard the folks at the scalp maintenance and haircutting department aren’t very reliable. Someone I know got a haircut there and ended up in trouble…”

“That’s probably just a communication problem. In my hometown, the streets are like that too. One wrong word and things go completely off track. So I usually bring a picture; that way, it’s clear how to understand it. It works really well.”

Peng, who works at RAISA, knows how important efficient information transfer is. Facing his colleague’s doubts, he didn’t back down but confidently pulled out a sample picture. “Did those people just explain it verbally before? Pictures carry way more information than words in this case. Watch me work, I’ll have you remember how to write the word ‘surprise’ in no time!”

In no time, he was already sitting in the chair at the scalp maintenance and haircutting department. “Yeah, just like this picture, shave both sides, then spike up the middle to make a cool rooster comb style, got it?”

“Got it!” The barber began his great creation. Both seemed determined, and half an hour later, Peng happily received a big gift.

“Your technique has a lot of flaws. From the side, it looks like a broom. You need to practice.” Peng looked in the small mirror and complained. Two seconds later, he realized something was wrong. Staring at the astonishing style in the mirror, he started questioning in disbelief, “Wait, tell me how you cut this? Who the hell taught you to cut it sideways?”

“I, I don’t know… I just knew you wanted this hairstyle. I didn’t really pay attention to the direction…”

Peng recalled all the previous details and suddenly realized he had overlooked one thing: this barber had a pair of cockfighting eyes that looked wise but actually showed poor vision and intelligence. Peng once thought such a ridiculous style could only exist in fiction. But most importantly, “Hey, can you turn around? Stop talking to that plastic mannequin! I’m right here!”

“Huh? Oh, sorry…” The barber finally moved his gaze away from the plastic mannequin and instead looked at the potted plant on the table.

Well, now there’s absolutely no hope left.
