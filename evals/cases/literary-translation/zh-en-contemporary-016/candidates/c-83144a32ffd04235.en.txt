Foundation, Site-17, Underground Level 4 High-Security Laboratory - Grand Auditorium

UTC-5, July 21, 2016, 10:00 AM

The Illusion Project was almost entirely run by O5-3 behind the Foundation’s back, so most of the members personally selected by O5-3 were Foundation personnel extremely loyal to him.

...However, when they saw on the giant projection screen occupying an entire wall of Site-17’s underground fourth floor a white-haired little girl in a sailor uniform drawn in anime style, speaking in a babyish voice and claiming to be the Foundation’s third Overseer, it was hard to guarantee that the loyalty of everyone present wouldn’t waver.

If it weren’t for O5-3 loudly maintaining order, the dozen or so tape recorders in the auditorium would probably have been overturned by cheers and screams. Although maintaining order in this form was basically ineffective, at least after about ten minutes he finally managed to quiet down the several hundred people present — then, she (at least now she could be called that) began explaining the process and expected outcomes of the plan:

“...So! This is the new path we are about to open! Imagine the surprises that could come from combining the advantages of the two-dimensional (anime) industry and the live streaming industry — especially since we have the first-mover advantage, our front-end can basically occupy and control the entire market!”

“This plan can first be implemented starting with those Groups of Interest (GoI), then gradually expanded outside the Veil. We expect the revenue (including derivative industries) to reach 500 to 600 billion within a few years, enough for us to maintain the Foundation’s operations without exhausting current resources...”

Judging from the audience’s reaction, O5-3 had never given such a successful speech before. After the speech ended, the anime character on the screen made a heart gesture to the audience, then disappeared into the darkness, leaving only cheers loud enough to ruin all the recording equipment in the auditorium.

“So your plan really is... anime virtual idols? Or are you going to appear on stage yourself?”

This was the 16th similar question O5-3 heard today, and his usual answers were simply “Yes,” “That’s right,” “Believe it or not,” “You can read the project plan yourself, it details the feasibility and our preliminary proposals,” and so on, until such questions stopped appearing altogether.

But considering the Foundation’s AI application technology and virtual scene construction technology, this was indeed a feasible plan — one that could rake in billions from the GoI. Especially after Fanta.AIC, created specifically for profit-making, replaced O5-3 as the action planner, the third Overseer’s path as an idol was truly irreversible.

On the first day of Dream God Live, the perfect 3D-rendered anime-style character designed based on AI aesthetics and meme propagation theory pulled several hundred million from the Western Dream God, plus a large number of derivative fan works;

Posters for the Illusion Project were plastered all over Alexylva University and the Deer Academy;

O5-3 (of course using an anime-style pseudonym, something like AiSaraChan) and the Wind Dew Inn’s first collaboration exploded the live scene of the Cambrian Seven-Day Tour;

The Broken Church (except for the Maxwell sect, who topped the gift charts on day one) and the Flesh Desire cult members were the least able to accept all this, until Fanta.AIC (under O5-3’s direct instruction, meaning “we can’t give up any potential profit source”) approved two new skins with completely different styles — one covered in gears and metal, the other with tentacle monsters on the back — which also drove them insane;

Not to mention the cooperation with Ambrose Restaurant, whose business grew exponentially even if prices doubled or they just printed avatars;
