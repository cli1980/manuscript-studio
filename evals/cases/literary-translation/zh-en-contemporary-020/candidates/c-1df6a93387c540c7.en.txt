“Put this...”“Right...”“Then…”

“Bang!” Liu Jinyue suddenly slammed the table, the loud noise drew everyone's attention to him.

“You said... what would happen if I put my blood into that iron lump?” Liu Jinyue stood up and asked the researcher across from him.

“...No one knows, you can try it yourself.”

“That sounds exciting.”

“Crazy...” the researcher muttered under his breath.

* * *

When the O5 Council approved his biological experiment application, Liu Jinyue entered the experimental area with a box of blood samples, the blood inside was still warm.

“This time it’s…”

“It’s No. 259.”

“Report the experiment time.”

“3:05 PM, the report form is completed, the blood sample has been checked and is correct, the blood cells are still active.”

The fluorescent lights on the ceiling illuminated the dark floor as Liu Jinyue stared blankly at the machine until his assistant tapped him on the shoulder, bringing him back to reality. He turned to look at the assistant.

……

“Insert the second tube of blood sample for refinement.”

Based on previous experiences, the biological experiment was equipped with more guards, and researchers were armed to prevent any bizarre creatures from emerging, spewing acid.

The soft sound of metal gears meshed followed the click of the machine starting up. When he returned to the observation room, Liu Jinyue inadvertently looked up at the fluorescent lights, and a sudden ringing in his ears exploded, cold sweat soaked his clothes, sticking to his back. His breathing became rapid, and suddenly he knocked over a cup beside him, as if he were tightly bound to the ground by thorns, his limbs felt as if they had no bones... The ringing in his ears swelled to the limit, then abruptly stopped—only the creaking of the machine remained.

His vision sank into darkness, the only clear thing was the machine and himself.

The machine's gears turned into grinding wheels, starting to crush him from the soles of his feet, continuously pulling him inward. Flesh tore, bones shattered, and all he could hear was the persistent “creak, creak.” Some went to find a doctor, while others repeatedly called Liu Jinyue's name, but it was all in vain; he was alone, writhing on the ground. When he finally managed to turn over, the entire world suddenly twisted, his vision split from the center, spilling forth colors he could not name. The table towered into the clouds, the door transformed into a gaping maw, and the crowd twisted into grotesque shapes. He curled up on the ground, his face almost crumpled into a ball. A shard of glass pierced his palm, but he felt nothing; the overhead light tube turned into a blade, cleaving him again, splitting him into half—one half existing, the other flowing into the machine, the ringing in his ears surged once more, and he lost track of how long it lasted before it receded like a tide.

Suddenly, he seemed to understand something. Without time to think about his bleeding palm, he staggered to his feet, grabbed the gun from the table, and rushed out of the observation room.

He guessed correctly: a person appeared where something was supposed to emerge.

But he did not expect it to be a woman.

The woman, like a newborn lamb, was completely naked, weak and bewildered, sitting in the output chamber.

Liu Jinyue's gaze met hers, and he saw her pupils dilate infinitely, filled with everything he feared, loved, hated, and mourned. In that brief moment, dozens of ways to stop his breath flashed through his mind, thoughts he often entertained, but now each one was clear enough to terrify him. Liu Jinyue could feel the connection between them... Yes, he could not accept another version of himself appearing before him.

He raised the gun, wanting to send this “self” into eternal sleep, but once again fell into unconsciousness.

His mind was left in chaos.
