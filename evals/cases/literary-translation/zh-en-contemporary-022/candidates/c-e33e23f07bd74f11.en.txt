Dear students, teachers, as well as the staff of the General Affairs and External Affairs departments, good morning.

First of all, I would like to express my sincere gratitude to all of you studying at this school and all of you working here: over these years, the reason this school has been able to stand firm through endless storms is entirely thanks to your unwavering support. Next, I would like to offer my heartfelt congratulations to everyone present: congratulations to you, the proud students of our school. From this day forward, the moment you step out of the school gates, you will officially bid farewell to campus life. Perhaps you will find a place where you can fully utilize your talents; perhaps you will enter another ivory tower to continue honing your skills; perhaps you will embark on an unknown path that no one can foresee. But in any case, you will leave this land of joy and step into an unknown world.

To describe this school as a "land of joy" might be a bit boastful, but it has been the goal that I, along with the teachers and staff, have strived for all these years. Using every resource and every technology available, we have built a place where you can live happily and learn joyfully. In my eyes, the moment I see your eyes full of hope for the future, I feel that my efforts have not been in vain.

Perhaps you have already heard—or rather, you didn’t even need to hear—since after your cohort enrolled, no new admissions have been made, which has long signaled everything. The world is undergoing a rapid transformation; perhaps the knowledge you have learned will become useless the very next day; perhaps one day you will have to face challenges you have never encountered before; or perhaps, at some point, you will look back and recall the bits and pieces of life in this small school that should have long since faded away.

Therefore, as your principal, at this very last moment, I offer you my blessings: I hope that after you leave, you will live a life without regrets; I hope your wisdom will be fully realized so that you can make the right choices; I hope you will always maintain an optimistic heart and face adversity with a smile; I hope you will have a will of steel and never do anything that you will regret for a lifetime.

All things return to nothingness, nothingness gives birth to all things, and the land of joy will eventually collapse. What you must do now is to wave goodbye to the land of joy in your heart and embark on a new journey.

May you find a new land of joy. Congratulations on your graduation.
