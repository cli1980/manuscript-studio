Repossessing a body felt unfamiliar. Bright stared into the green eyes of the one in the mirror, still somewhat stunned.

It was made according to the earliest memories in your deep consciousness, Machine explained. We can only dig this deep, it might look not that much of alike.

"It's good enough," he shrugged.

What do you plan on doing next?

"Coming this far, what about giving me an interstellar spaceship?"

* * *

While Machine recreates the aerospace industry with a magnificent product line, Bright became a completely idled personnel, hanging around everywhere everyday in this metallic world. The atmosphere of the planet is thin, and the composition much different from on Earth. When the particle flows from deep within space impacts the surface of the planet, it forms a truly splendid scenery.

In the wind there were deep, soft electromagnetic waves, seemingly something enormous has shot through the night sky, rustling.

Some day he lied on the ground, looking at the stars, just as always. A voice lightly pushed him in his brain. Compared to the previous, this consciousness is softer, with warmth which is different from Machine, which made Bright a bit surprised.

We still have the difference in sex, the voice explained in chatterings. You are now talking to 4.2% of the total population, most of whom are females.

There's something you should know. We have found that your amulet still holds onto all the consciousness that have been wiped away. Though only in pieces, they have always been with you.

You have brought the museum of humanity with you, child.

Heheh, don't be eager to argue. Speaking of time, you are the ancestor of all living human beings; but as a prehistoric being, suddenly finding yourself under the stars in the era of galaxies, would you not have ignited the hesitance and curiosity of a child?

Another wave of consciousness arises in resonance. Our branch has settled in this galaxy for too long, there's no turning back for us. But you, you can still find the way to set sail.

Go dive into the sea of stars, go find the rest of man kind, go explore the entirety of the universe, go look at the borderless scenery.

Oh child, our child, their soft voices resounded in his head, may starlight shine along your path forward.

……

The day has come when it's time to begin the journey.

One last question, Machine asked. What's your name?

"Is this utterly important?" Bright asked them back.

It decides the way you would stay on our records.

Bright thought for a while.

"At the time when I was still a human…they called me Jack Bright, Doctor or Human Resources Supervisor. In the records of the Foundation, I was SCP-963, 'Immortality' —— but I'm tired of immortality."

He slowly, thoughtfully said, "there was a number that has long been abolished, they called her 'Child of Man'. I believe, you could also call me…"

The man who was a human went silent for a moment.

Machine waited patiently.

"'Soul of Man'."

The ground beneath his feet tremored away. In almost less than a breath, the dark night sky surged, just like the millions of times he fell into the embrace of the amulet. Only this once, he didn't feel cold anymore.

Soul of Man falls into deep stars.
