This relationship meant the threat could not be taken away, and their conspiracy could not succeed—but now their assault team was struggling to achieve this. The labyrinth's network forces also began to intervene; after all, Silicon Universe City was their turf, and they had to manage this mess.

At the center of this rushing tide, her body had long been destroyed, her right arm torn off—the so-called torrent was actually one of the manifestations of ultra-high-density information data. This information was merging, compressing, expanding, colliding, and fusing.

Yet she still raised her head and waved her still intact left hand. Before the lightning pierced her chest, before the light obscured the entire sphere, her face always wore a smile and always looked at Jin Zhan.

As swift as birth, disappearance was only a moment. The massive overload information instantly aggregated and collapsed, forming a dazzling wormhole. The entire supernatural threat was completely crushed, reduced to basic data information, and dragged along with this girl into a time-and-space-spanning abyss.
