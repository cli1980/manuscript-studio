You are an archivist at the Foundation. You don’t know what time it is now; the office lights have already been turned off. But it doesn’t matter—you’re not sleepy anyway. You continue organizing the documents assigned to you. It’s routine work: syncing with the main site’s database, checking update logs, supplementing links to related research, and inputting some handwritten research reports into the archive. Daily operations.

At this moment, you are staring at the Foundation’s K-Class Incident database. This isn’t the first time you’ve organized this list, and the update speed is much faster than you expected. The Foundation’s researchers truly have a keen eye, discovering new anomalous effects that could potentially affect all humanity from time to time. You can’t help but feel that humans are incredibly fragile, especially from the Foundation’s perspective. A slight mistake, a containment failure, and the magnificent civilization we have built over millennia could collapse in an instant. You scroll through the descriptions of each K-Class scenario and their sometimes straightforward, sometimes obscure names. Finally, your gaze stops at the second-to-last item on the list. You let out a bitter smile.

Scenario Number: ΨK-Class

Probability: Occurred

Severity: Survivable, but requires moderate adjustment to human civilization.

Description: A ΨK-Class (“Death Exhaustion”) scenario refers to a situation where humans (or all life) find it difficult to die. In this scenario, biological death is not entirely impossible, but the conditions for death are extremely stringent, while the conditions for survival are unusually simple. All protocols for this scenario assume that the Foundation has taken measures to prevent a Broken Veil event. It is also assumed that the Foundation maintains its current structure and branches.

Note that the ΨK-Class scenario may be a precursor to an ΩK-Class (“Death Termination”) scenario.

Priority:

1. Veil Control: The norm has been redefined. Modified research reports have been disseminated to explain the effects of the ΨK-Class scenario. Veil control is in good condition, public acceptance of the ΨK-Class scenario is high, and containment continues as usual.

2. Death Condition Research: Research on death conditions is progressing well. Although death conditions are more stringent than the previous norm, they remain controllable. Civilian participation in research has been permitted to improve efficiency.

This is not the first time you’ve seen this entry. Every time you see the three blue words “Occurred,” you feel as if you glimpse the helplessness of the first researcher who wrote them. Sometimes you wonder, if a K-Class scenario has already occurred, does it still need to be recorded in the database? The Foundation does so for now, perhaps because humanity has not yet been utterly defeated—only that we need to try harder than ever before.

Just then, you notice an update to this entry that you hadn’t seen before.

Trigger:

> Research Report R-ΨK-19032135.doc

You are slightly startled. A research report linked here must mean a breakthrough discovery. If the Foundation has thoroughly uncovered the trigger for the ΨK-Class scenario, then perhaps the antidote to reverse it is within reach. The Foundation never gives up. Contain, Secure, Protect. The Foundation lives by this motto. You feel a glimmer of hope. You even feel a bit proud to be part of the Foundation.

With a small surge of excitement, you click the link. A popup appears.

By order of the Overseer Council

This document is restricted to Yellowstone clearance holders only
Unauthorized access prohibited

.
.
.

System did not detect Yellowstone clearance
If you believe you have Yellowstone clearance, please click here to manually enter credentials

Regrettably, you do not have the corresponding clearance. You don’t even know what “Yellowstone clearance” is. The Foundation holds many secrets, and you are just an ordinary archivist, destined to be excluded from many of them. You have long known and gotten used to this. Some things are better left unknown. You’re not a fan of Lovecraft, but you feel that, at least for yourself, you shouldn’t sail too far or delve too deep.

Besides, even if you knew the truth, what would it matter? The world has already changed, the norm has been altered. We must try very hard just to die without effort.

The truth no longer matters, does it?
