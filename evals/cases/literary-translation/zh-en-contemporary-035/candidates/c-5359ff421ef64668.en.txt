Jane said that before becoming a member of the Foundation, she was a United Nations reporter.

Once, she followed a UN peacekeeping force to South Africa, but got separated from them along the way. Not only did Jane lose contact with the peacekeepers, she also got lost in the forest. She relied on a compass to navigate through the woods and left marks along the way. Half a day passed, and as dusk fell, Jane still hadn’t found a way out.

She had a flare stick in her backpack, but the forest was too dense. To prevent the light from being blocked by leaves, she chose to find a relatively open area to ignite the flare stick.

Jane recalled that she lit the flare stick beside a small hill where there were obvious signs of human activity—some trees were scorched, and there were yellow seals and an abandoned campsite in the woods. After confirming the weather was clear, Jane lit the flare stick and used it to ignite the campfire at the site.

It seemed that Jane attracted something unexpected, though she couldn’t quite remember clearly. She only recalled that the shadow of that thing in the forest looked like a bear. So she played dead by lying down inside an abandoned tent. But while lying there, she made an unexpected discovery: inside the tent’s storage bag, she found a signal pistol. After hesitating several times, she quietly poked her head out and fired a signal flare into the sky.

As the flare slowly rose, the area around, shrouded in darkness, became visible. Under the eerie red light of the flare, Jane finally saw the strange creature clearly. It was twisted, writhing, and roaring. It seemed afraid of the light and retreated back into the forest.

Summoning her courage, Jane stepped toward the campfire, picked up the still-burning flare stick, and raised it above her head. Jane said that at that moment she felt truly desperate, thinking she couldn’t escape, so she decided to take the risk and use the flare stick to drive the monster away.

But the creature didn’t seem afraid of the flare stick in her hand. It slowly emerged from the darkness, looking very creepy under the red light of the signal flare. Jane’s mind went blank; she wanted to call for help but couldn’t make a sound. She took a few steps back, fell to the ground, and then lost consciousness.

As for what happened afterward, after some thought, Jane told us that she returned safely to the UK and received an anonymous invitation. Perhaps something happened during the period before her memory was wiped, which is why she was told she could no longer live under her previous identity. However, aside from being unable to see her family, this didn’t seem to affect Jane’s life much.

“I later heard that it was a joint operation between the Foundation and the GOC... Luckily, it wasn’t the GOC people who found me, otherwise they wouldn’t have spared me, an I-class reality bender,” Jane said, patting her head somewhat embarrassed.

“Actually, I never really understood what reality bending means... that’s what the people who did my physical exams told me. So far, besides discovering that I can’t use adjectives, they haven’t found any superpowers in me...”
