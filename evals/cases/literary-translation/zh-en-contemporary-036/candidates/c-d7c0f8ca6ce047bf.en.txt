Early in the morning, a girl carrying a fishing rod, a toolbox, and a plastic bucket signed in at the guard hall of the No. 77 special external station. Then she walked toward the nearby reservoir power station, humming a little tune along the way, searching in the small woods for the river she had discovered yesterday.

“From the small hill, walk west a hundred and twenty steps, through the bamboo thicket, hear the sound of water, like the tinkling of jade pendants, my heart is delighted...”

The girl found a good spot to sit down, took out her fishing rod, and from the toolbox took out a fishhook, a float, and bait. After complaining about the strong smell of her bait, she skillfully cast the rod, throwing the baited hook into the river.

Looking around at this beautiful little world, the girl couldn’t help but continue reciting.

“Green trees and lush vines, intertwined and swaying, unevenly brushing. In the pool, there could be a hundred or so fish... A hundred or so fish? Where are a hundred or so fish???”

The girl noticed movement in the float, excitedly reeled in the line, and a lively silver little body leapt out of the river.

“A crucian carp... but so small, never mind, better let it go.” The girl removed the small crucian carp from the hook and threw it back into the river. Then she prepared to cast again.

“Today I have to catch at least twenty or thirty pounds, otherwise it’ll be hard to explain to the cafeteria,” the girl thought, recalling how she was scolded by the regional director two days ago for replacing the cafeteria’s flour with cement dust. She grew impatient and angrier the more she thought about it, and began planning her next prank.

“Add mustard mint to the cookies? No originality; cut the power? Might be a bit too much; or blow a hole in the dam? Uh, what am I thinking.” The girl gripped her fishing rod tightly, trying to calm herself down. After a while, she felt something pulling on the rod—it seemed to be a big fish, quite strong. She tried to wrestle with it to tire it out, but the “fish” didn’t budge at all.

“Bang!” The fishing line snapped, and the girl fell to the ground, hitting her head.

“Ow!” The girl struggled to stand up, annoyed by this “fish.” She dropped the fishing rod, took out an electric telescopic rod from her toolbox, and approached the riverbank. She extended the rod into the river and put on protective goggles.

Sparks flickered in her hands, crackling loudly. Lightning continuously generated from her hands and was conducted into the river through the telescopic rod. The girl, staying within a safe range, jabbed the electrified rod randomly into the water.

Suddenly, bubbles rose from the water, and then a scout wearing a Foundation frogman suit twitched as he swam to the surface. Seeing this, the girl quickly stopped electrifying the water.

“This is bad.” The girl squatted down, took out her phone to take a photo, preparing to post on her blog: “But this one should weigh about two hundred pounds!”

“Misaka!!!” The captain’s angry shout came through her earpiece, but she ignored it, took off the earpiece, pulled the frogman out of the water, and dragged him step by step back to the No. 77 special external station.

“Caught a big fish today!”
