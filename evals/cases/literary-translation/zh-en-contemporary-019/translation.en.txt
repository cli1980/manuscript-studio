Marnton Chrysos lit a cigarette at his workstation to do nothing, as always. Perhaps to people like him, the best remedy was to lazily sink into his char. Yet, the moment he opened his eyes, he once again saw the unsettling fluorescent tube.

Just like the lighting that day……

That was a few decades ago, when he was still a child.

One day, he stared at the grey cloud through the window, and his mother came over.

“What are you looking at, did something bad happen?”

“Oh… nothing.”

His mother saw through him, but she didn't try to pry further.

“If anyone bullied you or something made you feel bad, would you come over and tell mommy?”

“Ok.”

Marnton Chrysos told her everything that happened in the school, although his problems may not be solved, at least he had a person to vent to.

“The teacher urged me to pay the material fee again today, and even told others to keep away from me because I didn’t like learning…”

“I arrived a bit late to the canteen today, and Ye Jian Hua scolded me for the whole day right away just because I blocked him…”

“Today…”

Another night, he set off from the after-school care program, and bought a lot of snacks. Although there was a thunderstorm raging outside, he still hopped onto the path he was familiar with, with thunder crashing every so often.

“Mommy will like these tomato-flavored, right?…” Walking down the road, he held a bag of snacks.

“Boom…” A loud thunder struck from the sky.

“Mommy must be happy about this!” Marnton Chrysos said while opening the door with the key.

“Boom.”

Waiting until the door opened with a squeaking sound, the scent of joss along with mahogany mixed flowed into his face, familiar still… isn’t it?

That scent contained something Marnton Chrysos was afraid of, not only alcohol, but that kind of “rust” scent. A thunder illuminated the dark living room; Marnton Chrysos could not forget the scene in front of him that day.

The mother lay on the sofa, not resting, since her chest wasn’t moving at all. She was just lying, the bloodstain on her neck seemed to be creeping onto the body, back, sofa, until it reached the ground… yet she didn’t even close her eyes.

“Boom!”

Father’s snores spread from the inside, so loud, it drowned out the roaring lightning.

So, Marnton Chrysos grabbed the knife covered in mother’s blood, and walked to the bedside.

…

Red and blue lights were flashing into Marnton's eyes. The alarm pierced his ears, and his brain went blank.

He murmured when he was on the road to the orphanage: “What am I left with? Ah, let me think… I got nothing left, I got myself left, nobody’s giving me anything and I’m not giving anything to others, I got nothing… I got nothing to worry about, I can do anything I want, then go further… until I do something irredeemable… and everything is over.”

“Nah, it costs a lot, you are paying for the excessive part? Everyone else can eat those, how about you just don’t go if you really can’t.”
