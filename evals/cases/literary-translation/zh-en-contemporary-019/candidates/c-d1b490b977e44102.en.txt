Liu Jinyue, as usual, lit a cigarette and idly sat at his workstation. For someone like him, lazily lying back in his chair was the best kind of healing. But when he opened his eyes, it was that unsettling fluorescent light again.

Just like the lightning that day...

That was decades ago, when he was still a child.

One day, he was staring out the window at the dark clouds when his mother came over.

"What are you looking at? Is something wrong?"

"Ah... nothing."

But his mother had already seen through him and didn’t press further.

"If anyone bullies you or something makes you unhappy, come tell mom, okay?"

"Okay."

So Liu Jinyue would tell his mother about things at school. Although it might not solve anything, at least he had someone to "vent" to.

"Today the teacher urged me to pay the material fee again and said other kids avoid me because I don’t like studying..."

"Today I was a bit late to the cafeteria, and Ye Jianhua scolded me all day because I blocked his way..."

"Today..."

Another night, after finishing school care, he bought a big bag of snacks. Even though there was a thunderstorm outside, he still hopped along the familiar path, with thunder occasionally booming in the sky.

"Mom would like the tomato flavor, right..." he said, carrying the snacks.

"Boom..." A thunderclap shook the sky.

"Mom will definitely be happy!" Liu Jinyue said as he took the keys out of his pocket to open the door.

"Boom."

When the door creaked open, the mixed scent of incense and rosewood hit him — still familiar... or was it?

That smell was mixed with things Liu Jinyue feared, not just the smell of alcohol but also the scent of "rust." A flash of lightning cut through the dim living room, and the scene before his eyes was something he could never forget.

His mother was lying on the sofa—not resting—her chest showed no rise or fall. She just lay there, with bloodstains on her neck seeming to crawl, spreading onto her body, her back, the sofa, and the floor... and she hadn’t even closed her eyes.

"Boom!"

From the inner room came his father’s loud snoring, louder than the thunder outside.

So, Liu Jinyue picked up the knife stained with his mother’s blood and walked to the bedside.

...

The blue and red lights kept assaulting Liu Jinyue’s retinas, the alarm ringing in his ears, his mind blank.

On the way to the welfare institute, he muttered to himself: "What do I still have? Ah... let me think... I have nothing left. I only have myself. No one can give me anything, and I won’t give anything to others either. I have nothing... nothing to worry about anymore. I can do whatever I want, and even go further... until I cause something unforgivable... and then it’s all over."
