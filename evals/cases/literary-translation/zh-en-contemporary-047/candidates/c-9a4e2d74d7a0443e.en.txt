Jasmine's appearance was not flashy. Under the cover of the audience's commotion, the keyboardist, drummer, guitarist, bassist, and DJ moved about in the darkness of the stage. After they took their positions, the venue's lights suddenly all turned on, pointing to the center of the stage. Jasmine, dressed in elegant clothes and wearing a headset microphone, quietly appeared on stage, greeting the wave of cheers with a smile.

"Hel—lo, eve—ry—one!"

The brief and awkward three words, spoken in Cantonese, pushed the already enthusiastic atmosphere to a new peak, then abruptly stopped at Jasmine's gesture.

"For this time, meeting with all of you, Ma Li Ka Jasmine, besides rehearsing, also worked hard to learn a bit of Chinese!"

"I've learned both Cantonese and Mandarin, to better understand each other!"

"This is not the first foreign language I've learned, nor will it be the last. One day, friends all over the world will overcome language bar...riers! And listen to me sing together!"

Although Jasmine's Cantonese was completely incoherent, full of lazy sounds, and she even forgot the words at one point, inside the venue, only her voice echoed, without a single noise disturbing her speech.

"I believe everyone has been waiting a long time, right?" Jasmine looked around the venue, happily listening to the sounds of agreement, and with a well-practiced sentence, opened the curtain for the performance: "Come on! The show begins! Sa a! Raibu hajimaru yo!"

Then, the muse goddess descended to the human world once again.

* * *

Zhou Meitu was not a fan of Jasmine. As a self-proclaimed "MK girl," she shouldn't have been chasing after female celebrities—unfortunately, the "male god" she was pursuing happened to be a big fan of Jasmine and had bought an extra ticket to invite her to watch the concert together. Thus, Meitu became one of the very few exceptions in the venue.

From this, it was clear that she should not have cared about the scene on stage, but rather would be scrolling through her phone to cater to her male god's interests while complaining to her friends. However, the sudden burst of music on stage pulled her gaze back to the stage.

Then Meitu's eyes could not leave Jasmine.

Jasmine's elegant outfit was bright but not dazzling, allowing people to calmly appreciate her appearance. Her face, lightly powdered, instantly made Meitu lose any desire to compare herself.

Meitu had never learned Japanese and could not understand the meaning of the lyrics Jasmine sang, but the voice coming from the stage easily evoked her emotions, blending her into the surrounding atmosphere.

Amid the heavy interplay of instrumentals, Jasmine moved her hands and feet to the rhythm, each movement precise, as if every lift of hand or foot appeared at the right time and place naturally. Even with such intense movement, the pleasant singing continuously flowed from her mouth, her breath not disturbed in the slightest.

"Love's Makiri," as Jasmine's main song, was not complicated in structure. The main and secondary parts alternated, lasting about four minutes. When the song ended, the accumulated frenzy in the venue instantly exploded. Zhou Meitu jumped up, tightly gripping the arm beside her, screaming in the shocked gaze of her male companion, calling out the idol's name—within just four minutes, another fan of Yazawa Jasmine was born.

Caught in the frenzy, Meitu, her successfully converted male companion, and other fans in the frenzy all failed to notice the anomaly on stage: Jasmine's face, while thanking fans, showed an unusual flush; the instruments held by the band members on stage were merely plastic replicas; and the dark clouds hovering over the Red Hall, covering the entire Kowloon Peninsula, silently and quietly disappeared...
