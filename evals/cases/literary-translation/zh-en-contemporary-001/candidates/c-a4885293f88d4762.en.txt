What was going on? Lyn hesitated about whether to step inside, but suddenly the video switched to a close-up of the Epidemic Doctor’s face, startling him so much that he slammed the door shut.

Damn it. Although Lyn thought this, having worked at the Foundation for so long, he had naturally encountered no shortage of strange things. So he comforted himself that this might just be a hallucination or a localized anomaly, took a deep breath, and opened the door again.

The video showed a glowing yellow giant bug.
