About ten minutes later, Lyn was walking back with five bags of potato chips and four bottles of drinks. The cafeteria wasn’t far, but karldark’s steak was naturally nowhere to be found—and to make matters worse, he’d even gotten an eye-roll from the cook. Still, the snacks in his hands should be enough to get him through the rest of the footage. Thinking this, Lyn pushed open the door.

The room was empty. Yet the footage was still playing.

“What the—?” Lyn quickly stepped back and checked the door. No mistake—this was the same room. He looked again. Chen’s empty chip bags were still scattered on the table.

What was going on? Lyn hesitated at the threshold, when suddenly the footage cut to a close-up of the Plague Doctor’s oversized head. Startled, he yanked the door shut.

This is insane. Lyn thought as much, but having worked at the Foundation for so long, he’d seen his fair share of strange things. He told himself it might just be a hallucination or a localized anomaly, took a deep breath, and pulled the door open again.

He nearly sighed with relief when he saw karldark and Darklight. But when his gaze shifted toward where Agent Chen should be, he found a flagpole standing upright on the couch.

The footage now showed a glowing, giant yellow bug.

The footage was filled with the same faceless figures.

Lyn pulled the door open.

His three colleagues were sitting on the ceiling, grinning down at him.

Lyn pulled the door open.

The room had turned into an ancient Greek style, with impossibly massive stone columns standing in each corner. Darklight held a scroll, dressed in a robe along with karldark. Chen was embedded in the floor.

Lyn pulled the door open.

This time, everything seemed normal.

Thank goodness. Relieved, Lyn was about to say something when Darklight looked up from his book.

“Milk four-legged fish doomsday,” he said.

* * *

Lyn pulled the door open.

The entire room was piled high with apple seeds.

* * *

Lyn pulled the door open.

A portrait of Dr. Pishop had replaced the projector, and a group of robed figures stood in a circle, chanting.

Candle flames flickered throughout the room.

* * *

What the hell is going on? Lyn didn’t know why he kept opening the door over and over. It was probably a futile attempt to reclaim his sanity, but he had even forgotten to consider options like leaving and reporting to his superiors.

This time, before him lay a grand hall far larger than the door’s frame. In the center, a giant green lizard with tough, scaly hide was giving some kind of speech. Next to him sat an old computer.

“So… humans, such disgusting humans, shall perish!” he roared, lashing his long tail behind him.

To Lyn’s horror, the hall was filled with various high-risk SCP entities, all cheering at 682’s speech. Holy hell—that terrifying old man who corrodes everything was just a few steps away.

Lyn was about to close the door when 682 turned his head and fixed his dozens of golden eyes on him.

“Ah,” he said. “It seems my black pepper steak has arrived.”

Lyn looked down. Black sauce was dripping from his body.
