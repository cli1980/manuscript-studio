Jasmine and the producer also left the room from the other side, walking side by side toward the venue— a special vehicle was already waiting for them in a hidden spot.

“That situation just now was a bit dangerous,” the producer sighed. The flush on Jasmine’s face hadn’t faded yet; it was a symptom of the backlash encountered while using the singing technique.

“It’s nothing! I’ve faced more dangerous situations than this before.”

“You’re different from us. You voluntarily chose to work with us as an outsider. If something happens to us, it’s one thing, but if something happens to you, the trouble we get into is minor compared to how guilty we’d feel.”

“I told you, it’s fine! When I signed with you, I already knew this job was more dangerous than being an ordinary idol. It’s okay! Mm!”

“You should at least take it seriously... In dangerous moments, you really could lose your life...”

In the empty corridor, the idol in plain clothes and the producer in black walked side by side. No one disturbed them, no one noticed them.

Suddenly, the producer seemed to remember something and turned to ask Jasmine:

“Speaking of which, unlike Kisaragi, who came from the supernatural school and had already learned sound strike techniques before joining Shancai and was already one of us, you, an art academy student who accidentally applied to us, why did you still choose to stay after learning the truth?”

“Hmm, what if I told you that I actually want to be an ordinary idol? How would you see that?”

“Like I said from the beginning, we can rewrite your memories and guide the audience’s perception. As long as you choose to quit, you’ll wake up as a well-known idol unrelated to us. You can still make that choice now.”

“Just kidding! Just kidding! I really did think about being an ordinary idol, but the experiences I’ve had are memories I can’t let go of. An ordinary Jasmine who just sings and dances isn’t a good Jasmine, hmm!”

“What you said... even without sound strike accompaniment, without sound strike techniques and singing methods, you’re still the best idol I’ve ever seen. Don’t belittle yourself so much.” The producer lightly tapped Jasmine’s head. As she playfully groaned and covered her head, he said to her again:

“The effect of this ceremony was better than expected, so the next two ceremonies are no longer necessary. For the next two days, just enjoy the performances as a top idol!”

“Yay!”

As the idol cheered, they reached the end of the corridor. Suddenly, Jasmine seemed to remember something and asked the producer:

“By the way, Producer-san, when you first told me we were a special task force of some foundation, I was really shocked! But isn’t this an entertainment agency? Why did you describe it like a special forces unit?”

“Hehe, because we do work like a special forces unit,” the manager laughed:

“When people in the foundation think of a special task force, they always imagine men in black and super soldiers, but there are always things they can’t handle and need us to solve. So why wouldn’t we be a special task force?”

The captain and producer of Mobile Special Task Force na-65 “Shancai Project,” along with his team member and ace idol, walked side by side out of the battlefield that belonged to idols.
