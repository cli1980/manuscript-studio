“I knew you’d be here.” Hel pushed open the door, and the cool evening breeze brushed against her cheek. The sun had long since set, and only the ocean of city neon lights illuminated Jin Zhan’s figure.

Hel stepped forward and, without paying much attention to the dazed woman, sat directly on the concrete steps beside her. Their legs dangled freely, completely ignoring the traffic 900 meters below. At the top of the building, they swayed gently in the wind. Neither of them spoke; they simply sat in silence, no eye contact exchanged, just letting the high-altitude wind whisper in their ears.

After a while, Jin Zhan broke the silence.

“Of course I’d be here,” she said.

* * *

“Where the hell is this?!” When someone’s blindfold is ripped off and they find themselves standing at the edge of a landmark skyscraper 900 meters high, that person would surely be terrified. Jin Zhan was exactly that kind of person. Taking in the magnificent view of the surrounding skyscrapers bathed in sunlight, she lost her balance and fell backward, plunging into the arms of the very person responsible for this whole situation.

“This? This is the rooftop of the United Nations Black Obelisk Building! What, you usually only look at it from below, so you don’t recognize it from this angle?” The girl behind Jin Zhan buried Jin Zhan’s face in her embrace, her face lit up with a radiant smile as she ran two fingers through Jin Zhan’s golden mushroom-cut hair.

“Who asked you that! I’m asking why you brought me here!” Jin Zhan lifted her head, her bangs just brushing the other girl’s nose. If Jin Zhan’s expression at that moment could be summed up in three letters, “QAQ” would be the perfect choice.

“Well, why?”

“I’m asking you!”

“Oh? Why?” The girl holding Jin Zhan tossed her long black hair, gently biting her index finger with her snow-white tiger teeth, her big bright eyes glancing left and right. “Isn’t it because you’re always stuck inside the base and never come out? I just wanted to take you out for some fun.”

After saying that, Jin Zhan immediately felt a strong push.

* * *

“She really pushed you off?” Hel looked down with an expression that was neither approving nor disapproving.

“Yeah! I fell right behind the Russian Internet Security Advisory Team! I was so embarrassed!”

“I’m going to check the security logs when I get back. Such an epic embarrassing moment can’t be missed.”

“Knock it off!”

* * *

“Really not going to try?” Seeing the pair of pure white wings shimmering with a faint blue glow before her, Jin Zhan pretended not to see them and busied herself reading an introduction she’d found in the employee handbook to distract herself.

“...Section Four: The United Nations Black Obelisk Building serves as the official United Nations office and diplomatic conference center within the I.D.S. As a symbol of the official representative status of the I.D.S., the Black Obelisk Building’s exterior is entirely clad in solemn black glass. The rectangular structure, combined with the black facade and the 1:4:9 ratio of its dimensions, symbolizes grandeur and steadfastness, while also borrowing elements from classic films to express a wish for humanity’s enduring prosperity...”

“Come on, you’re not going to be tested on this.” Jin Zhan chose to ignore the voice again, but the source of the sound was oddly placed. She turned her head toward the sky—the direction the voice came from—and her mouth dropped open in surprise. The wings had already grown on that person’s back, beating strong gusts of wind in the air. The pure white feathers spanned over five meters, their pale blue glow somewhat dimmed in the sunlight. Yet the snowflake-like shimmer was crystal clear. Feathers mixed with snowflakes drifted down, as if the wings themselves were fleeting clouds descending to the world.

In the center of the drifting clouds, under the clear holy light, the angel revealed the most beautiful smile.

The angel then bent her left knee slightly, her right toe lightly touching the edge of the concrete step beside Jin Zhan. Her whole body tilted backward at a 45º angle, supported by the lift of her wings so she didn’t fall. Her left hand reached out to the dazed Jin Zhan; the pure white fingers, like a budding snow lotus, exuded a gentle fragrance in front of the golden-haired girl.

“Don’t you really want to try?”

Jin Zhan grasped the hand before her, feeling for the first time what it meant to be “blushing.”
