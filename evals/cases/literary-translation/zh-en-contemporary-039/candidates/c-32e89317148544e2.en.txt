A girl opens her eyes, awakening in this world like a paradise.  
She dazedly gazes at the sky, dazzling like shattered glass; on the vast meadow, the wind blows, making the knee-high tall grass sway, carrying the clouds of the sky, flowing toward the distance.

“Where is this…”  
Then, a soap bubble appears in her hand. She pokes it but cannot burst the bubble.  
Behind her, hundreds and thousands of such soap bubbles gather into a rainbow-colored ocean, flying into the sky with the wind.  
Isn’t it beautiful?

— Magic.  
The word instinctively pops into the girl’s mind, unsure whether it is an adjective or a noun. But since this place is so dreamlike, then magic must be an understandable concept here, right?  
The girl tidies her flaxen hair and begins to play in this field. She lavishly uses her creativity and imagination, freely displaying her talents on this magical continent where wishes come true. The soap bubbles behind her keep bubbling up from the soil, drifting toward the sky.

If only such times would never end.
