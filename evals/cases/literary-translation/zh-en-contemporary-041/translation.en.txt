Reality bender.

As the name suggests, they possess the ability to alter reality. To them, the established present could be shaped as if a piece of clay.

——And that’s exactly why, for a “future” that has failed to become a reality, it could never be changed.

The frail. Her ability is powerful, too powerful that even the God of Fate is attracted through her wish.

Therefore, her wish, will be fulfilled by fate itself.

“Ahhhh……Help……Me……”

The girl tries to seek help still, from her hoarse voice.

She’s already heard the unending gunshots and shouts outside, until those sounds fade away slowly. She has realized a fact, a horrific fact: Someone killed all the guards just to get to herself.

“I ask of you, are you the one calling for me?”

The girl sits at a loss, staring at the “prince” breaking in through the wall……

Uh, not a prince.

She’s a white-haired sister.

“Hm……nice to meet you?” She greets. “Call me Skuld.”

And Skuld walks to the front of the bed.

“In a nutshell: Lemme hold you, so we get out of here.”

She does not catch up. In astonishment, the girl who call herself Skuld reaches out her hand:

“Our dear princess, what’re you waiting for?”

“Eh?”

And Skuld pulls her up right off the bat. She rushes out of the containment unit the girl had long since her childhood.

She feels the warmth in Skuld’s arm, a warmth different to any other. No one hugs her even before she falls to the coma, as if they’re fearful of her ability……

But this one’s different——that’s what instinct told her.

She turns around to look at the number plate behind Skuld’s shoulder. The plate reads:

SCP-239

“And so there wasn’t a magic school.”

She reliefs. Yet she feels sad in a sudden.

……Perhaps, her childhood fades out already at this very moment.

* * *

“Don’t move, I’m healing you with spells.”

In the camper they snatched somewhere, Skuld scrolls through messages while casting thaumaturgy on Sigurrós to revive mobility.

“You’re laying down for too long, quite normal to have muscles atrophied——Though as a reality be……recon——Ugh, just use reality bender.”

Skuld throws the phone onto sofa in annoyance.

“As a reality bender, an important thing is……um, your heart to believe. It’s magic of your own, so you have to trust hard that you move regularly. That’s how you get out of this unlucky situation.”

Sigurrós remains silent. She simply nods in a smile, and slowly picks up The Little Prince to read.

Then, she glances at Skuld. She wonders what is the “prince” that brought her out from the unchanging “white room” like, she looks only a bit older than her. But Sigurrós only see her pulling out a guitar from nowhere.

“Is this……a guitar? My mentor likes to play a guitar ’bit smaller than this one.”

“Ugh……that’s called an Ukulele.”

Skuld sighs to correct Sigurrós, before adjusting the strings.

Skuld looks up, and asks her: “Any songs you like?”

Sigurrós thinks a bit, and suggested a rather simple one:

“I want……something I haven’t heard!”

“Surely.”

Skuld strums the strings. She begins to play a vaporwave genre, acoustic.

While Sigurrós falls to a reverie, in the dreamful, ethereal music.

“So, actually not a bad person……”

* * *

The frail opens her eyes. She wakes up in the world, a world that was once a paradise.

She stares at the horizon that had long since changed: Not the unending light anymore, but a flake of orange in dusk, shining onto her from a sun behind clouds; wind blows in the vast prairie still, bringing a wisp of warmth in daylight; no longer fluttering bubbles above the air, though she could create one or two as long as she wants.

It was beautiful, wasn’t it?

……Perhaps.

“At least, better than an unchanging light!”

The frail smiles in dream, unchained.
