# Translation-output quality case: zh-en-ciqing-001

This case assesses an English translation of the complete supplied short story
《刺秦》. It uses the existing `literary-translation-quality` format consumed by
the review server and judge runner.

## Input and output contract

| Role | Artifact |
|---|---|
| Input: source | Shared UTF-8 Chinese text and its source/revision metadata |
| Input: candidate | Complete English translation in `translation.en.txt`, or an explicit `--translation-file` |
| Input: assessment rules | `literary-translation-quality-v1`; the automated runner supplies its independent judge skill |
| Output: human assessment | Scores, rationale, and evidence-backed issues in `judgments.jsonl` |
| Output: model assessment | Predictions and run metadata under `evals/results/` |

The candidate is an **input to evaluation**, not something generated by the
judge. This case does not ingest DOCX, invoke production skills, orchestrate
approvals, or produce a workflow export. A candidate can come from a direct skill
run, a workflow run, or another explicitly recorded source.

The [separate workflow case](../../../../workflow/tests/cases/translation-ciqing-001/README.md)
starts with the raw DOCX and expects preparation, review receipts, accepted
chapters, and export provenance. Its manifest has a different task and
input/output structure. A direct production-skill execution likewise has its own
contract: verified prepared chapters and continuity context in; approved English
chapters and updated glossary/voice/domain records out. This quality-case format
assesses the resulting text; it is not a full skill-execution test specification.

## Literary quality checks

Use the existing 1–5 rubric for fidelity, English naturalness, literary voice,
and overall quality. Record evidence from both texts rather than prescribing
expected wording or scores.

- **Narrative levels:** preserve transitions between the contemporary first-person
  narrator, conversations with 静姐, and the embedded historical narrative.
- **Identity and address:** preserve the contextual relationship between 雒夫人
  and 函公主; keep 蒙, 莒, 鞠武, and 田光 distinct.
- **Disclosure:** preserve what letters, recollections, dialogue, and conjecture
  establish at each point. Do not turn belief into certainty or reveal information early.
- **Revisions within the fiction:** do not harmonize the narrator's changes to
  the embedded story as though they were accidental continuity errors.
- **Cultural language:** preserve instruments, titles, political relationships,
  and allusions without invented exposition or external historical corrections.
- **Restraint and rhythm:** retain repetition, pauses, dialogue boundaries, and
  the ending's limited outward action without adding motives or explanations.

These notes support human assessment. The automated judge receives only the
Chinese source, English candidate, and its independent judge skill; it does not
receive this README, workflow traces, or the production translation instructions.

## Assess a candidate

Supply the complete story's English text, not only the first translated section:

```powershell
.\.venv-workflow\Scripts\python.exe evals/run_judge.py `
  --mode judge `
  --case zh-en-ciqing-001 `
  --translation-file "C:/Evaluation/candidate.en.txt" `
  --model YOUR_JUDGE_MODEL
```

See the [judge setup](../../../README.md#run-the-translation-judge) for provider
configuration. Judge mode can assess an external candidate for this placeholder
case without reading human ratings or changing `translation.en.txt`. The whole
text must fit the model's context. Empty candidates are rejected.

For browser review, explicitly select a candidate for `translation.en.txt`,
record its provenance and revision, and restart the review server. Preserve
independent human assessments in `judgments.jsonl`; never store model scores there.
Mark the case ready only when the candidate, source review, and required rights
metadata are complete. Keep all excerpts and variants in source group
`short-story-ciqing` to avoid dataset-split leakage.
