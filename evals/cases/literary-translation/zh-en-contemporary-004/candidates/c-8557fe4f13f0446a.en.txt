Six

The upper-level narrative’s final stroke was already filled with vexed fury, decisively and swiftly throwing Vodka back to the starting point at that moment.

“I’ll only accompany you this last time...!” After being manipulated for so long, Vodka also had a temper, and couldn’t help but feel a raging anger in his heart.

He almost angrily opened his eyes and saw: 173’s entire body was covered with photos of 096 — indestructible, tightly packed, leaving no gaps.

“Hey, isn’t this a bug? I can’t see 173 at all! Now 173 can completely ignore human sight and go on a killing spree!” Vodka thought this was too much, utterly shameless.

The upper-level narrative awkwardly shifted a few photos to reveal a little bit of the earth-yellow shell.

After examining it for a long time, Vodka sighed.

He really had no other way; with his personal strength, this was as far as he could go. Maybe he could cut open an artery and use blood to paste over the photos, but there was no need to go that far, because this was only his personal failure, not the Foundation’s failure.

Whether it was temporarily assembling a containment box with airlifted armor plates, or spraying and fixing with fast-solidifying foam material, as long as a few D-class lives were spent to temporarily restrain 173, the Foundation would have countless ways to lock 173 up afterward.

But Vodka wouldn’t tell the upper-level narrative this, because there was no need to argue with God; it was better to let the other party be happy, otherwise this matter would never end.

After all, this was just... an idea mixed from two simple new urban legend creatures. The real world was far more complex and diverse, with many more interesting things. We would always have better ideas. There was no need to stay stuck in this story forever.

“...I lost.” Pretending to have thought it over for a long time, Vodka helplessly and wistfully announced.

The upper-level narrative didn’t reply, but Vodka could feel the other’s smug emotion between the lines.

“...Why are you so obsessed with 173 and 096? There are other combinations to experiment with, like anti-meme plus 173, or 096’s forced-spread meme, also 682 plus 035, and Yadabor’s Broken God, 3125 plus Xiangxiao demon or something...” Vodka spoke without much hope, trying to divert the upper-level narrative’s attention so it wouldn’t torment him with 173+096 anymore.

The air grew silent, and after a long while with no response, Vodka thought the upper-level narrative was no longer here.

“Great idea! How did I not think of that?!” Then a delighted voice echoed throughout the entire narrative.

Oh damn, screw you. Vodka cursed, rolled his eyes, then voluntarily closed his eyelids, only hoping not to open them again.
