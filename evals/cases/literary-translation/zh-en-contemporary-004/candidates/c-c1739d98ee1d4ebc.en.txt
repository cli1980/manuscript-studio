Six

The upper narrative's pen strokes were already brimming with fury and embarrassment as it swiftly and decisively hurled Vodka back to that very first moment.

I'll only indulge you this one last time…! After being pushed around for so long, Vodka had developed a temper of his own, and couldn't help the boundless rage surging in his heart.

He opened his eyes, practically fuming, and looked: 173's entire body was covered in photographs of 096—the indestructible kind, fitted perfectly together, leaving not a single gap.

"Hey, isn't this a bug? I can't see 173 at all! Now 173 can completely ignore human line of sight and go on a killing spree!" Vodka felt this was simply too outrageous, utterly shameless.

The upper narrative had no choice but to awkwardly shift the positions of a few photographs, revealing a tiny bit of mustard-yellow shell.

After studying it for a long while, Vodka sighed.

He truly had no way out now. With his personal strength alone, this was as far as he could go. Perhaps he could slit an artery and smear the photographs with blood to obscure them, but there was no need to go that far—because this was merely his personal failure, not the Foundation's.

Whether airlifting armor plates to assemble a temporary containment box on the spot, or using fast-solidifying foam materials to spray and fix it in place, as long as they spent a few D-class lives to temporarily immobilize 173, the Foundation would have countless ways to lock 173 away afterward.

But Vodka wouldn't tell the upper narrative this, because there was no need to contest with God. Better to let it have its fun, otherwise this thing would never end.

After all, this was just… an idea cobbled together from two simple new anomalous creatures. The real world was far more complex and diverse than this, with so many more interesting things out there—we would always have better ideas. We wouldn't, and needn't, remain forever stuck in this story.

"…I lose." After pretending to weigh and deliberate for a long time, Vodka announced with feigned helplessness and dejection.

The upper narrative gave no reply, but Vodka could feel its smug satisfaction emanating from between these very lines.

"…Why are you so fixated on 173 and 096? There are other combinations to experiment with, right? Like an antimeme plus 173, or 096 plus a compulsively contagious meme, or 682 plus 035, and Yaldabaoth plus Mekhane, 3125 plus the Hanged King's Phantom…" Vodka spoke without much hope, trying to divert the upper narrative's attention, to get it to stop tormenting him with the 173+096 combo.

The air fell silent. After a long while with no response whatsoever, Vodka thought the upper narrative was no longer here.

"Great idea! How did I not think of that?!" Immediately, a delighted voice resounded through the entire narrative.

Oh shit, go fuck yourself. Vodka cursed, rolled his eyes, and then actively closed his eyelids. He could only hope he wouldn't have to open them again.
