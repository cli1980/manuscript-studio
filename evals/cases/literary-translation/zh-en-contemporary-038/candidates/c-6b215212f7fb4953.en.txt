Year ███, Month ██, Day █, 00:04, Site-██

The currently confirmed facts about the High-Level Narrators are: First, no matter what, the narrations of the High-Level Narrators proceed according to certain settings. Narrations that violate these settings are almost never allowed. Second, the narrators’ narrations should have a targeted audience. This gives their narrations logical self-consistency. Third, by comparing the design styles of different anomalous documents, we have found that there is more than one narrator. According to incomplete statistics, there are currently at least ten different narrators creating K-class scenarios and containment failures for us...

The large screen projected an investigation report from the China branch. The Foundation’s senior staff at headquarters silently watched the report.

“Isn’t this strange?” Suddenly, Dr. Clef said, “Those damn narrators clearly see us discussing how to push them into the abyss, yet those [censored profanity] just won’t move their noble pens to trap us all with high-level narration?”

The words on the large screen flickered several times. Everyone’s gaze was drawn to the suddenly flickering screen. Under everyone’s watchful eyes, the screen switched to a blank document. Then, line by line, blue text appeared on the screen.

You know? When a puppet is controlled by a puppeteer, it may live a miserable life, but it can survive. When one day it breaks free from the puppet strings, what awaits it is not freedom, but destruction.

“See, that’s those damn narrators,” Dr. Clef turned around and said, “I’m sure their narrations have to cover all bases, but we don’t have to. When they have no narration, we can do whatever we want, unrestricted. But they can’t.”

“Now, next is the discussion about methods to break the barriers between narrative layers...”
