# Specialist Reviews

Use this workflow for focused expertise that complements, but does not impersonate, developmental editing, copyediting, legal counsel, or a lived-experience reader.

## Route and qualify the request

Identify the requested specialist lens and its completion scope:

- factual verification or research review;
- sensitivity, representation, or authenticity review;
- permissions and copyright-risk inventory;
- legal-risk signal review;
- focused continuity, technical, historical, cultural, or domain review.

State the available evidence, the reviewer's limitations, and what qualified human expertise may still be required. A model-generated review is not legal advice and must not claim lived experience, community authority, or professional credentials it does not possess.

Bind the review to the Git commit SHA and exact scope. Whole-book claims for permissions, recurring representation, or systemic factual consistency require the complete manuscript.

## Review method

For factual work:

- separate verified fact, plausible inference, authorial invention, and unresolved claim;
- prefer current primary or authoritative sources when external research is needed;
- cite sources near the relevant finding and record access dates when useful;
- flag contested, time-sensitive, or jurisdiction-dependent claims.

For representation or authenticity work:

- identify the represented people, context, narrative function, and likely reader impact;
- distinguish textual evidence from interpretation;
- avoid declaring a portrayal universally authentic, harmless, or offensive;
- identify patterns, power dynamics, stereotypes, omissions, and context risks;
- recommend a qualified sensitivity or authenticity reader when lived or community knowledge is material.

For permissions or legal-risk signals:

- inventory quotations, lyrics, images, brands, real people, private facts, allegations, and other potentially protected or harmful material;
- distinguish an editorial flag from a legal conclusion;
- escalate material risk to qualified counsel or a permissions professional.

## Verdict and proposals

Return the verdict before any intervention. Include scope, method, findings, evidence, confidence, limitations, unresolved questions, and escalation recommendations.

Create proposal IDs only when a bounded textual alternative is responsible and useful. Include exact current and proposed text, rationale, source support, tradeoffs, and status. Do not erase identity, dialect, conflict, or historical reality merely to reduce discomfort. When the correct resolution depends on the author's purpose or specialist judgment, issue a query instead.

## Approval and completion

Do not change the manuscript until the author approves exact proposal IDs. Revalidate the reviewed commit and current text, apply only accepted proposals, and commit the approved batch.

Mark the review complete only for its named lens and declared scope. A specialist verdict does not certify legal clearance, factual perfection, or universal cultural acceptance.
